# My favorite movie

## Spider-Man

 -The Amazing Spider-Man 2 is a visually stunning entry in the Spider-Man film legacy. It follows Peter Parker as he balances his life as a hero with his relationship with Gwen Stacy, all while facing the emergence of powerful new enemies like Electro

### Why I like it:

- Best live-action suit design: The vibrant red and blue with those massive, expressive white lenses is iconic.

- Top-tier web-swinging physics: The opening sequence captures the weight and momentum of swinging through NYC perfectly.

- Incredible chemistry: The dynamic between Andrew Garfield and Emma Stone feels grounded and authentic.

- Spectacular visual effects: The slow-motion "Spidey-sense" scenes and the electrical combat are a feast for the eyes.

### My favorite character: 

- Peter Parker / Spider-Man

### Quote that I like: 

- "I like to think Spider-Man gives people hope."
